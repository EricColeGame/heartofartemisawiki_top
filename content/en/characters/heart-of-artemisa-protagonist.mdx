## Rhythm-Driven Combat: Maximizing the Beat Multiplier

At the core of controlling the **heart of artemisa protagonist** is the beat-matching combat loop. While you can physically initiate strikes slightly outside the musical meter, doing so dulls your blade, lowers damage output, and leaves you vulnerable to enemy counterattacks. Conversely, maintaining a sustained sequence of on-beat inputs rewards you with devastating critical strikes and superior crowd control.

Attacking on tempo triggers audio-visual feedback that validates your accuracy. The hero strikes with greater force, staggered enemies suffer hit-stun, and room clears accelerate dramatically.

```
[Off-Beat Input]  ──> Weak Damage  ──> Loss of Momentum ──> Vulnerability
[On-Beat Input]   ──> Max Damage   ──> Stun Effects    ──> Rhythm Streak Multiplier
```

To optimize the protagonist's efficiency, study how input accuracy translates into combat effectiveness:

| Timing Tier | Input Window Window Offset | Damage Output | Additional Effects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Perfect Beat** | Within ±35 ms of pulse | 150% – 200% Base | Staggers standard foes, builds streak gauge |
| **Good Beat** | Within ±75 ms of pulse | 100% Base | Standard hit, maintains active combo streak |
| **Off-Beat** | Outside ±75 ms window | 35% – 50% Base | Breaks streak, locks out special weapon arts |
| **Missed Beat** | Action skipped during locked beat | 0% | Restores baseline positioning, risks taking hits |

Maintaining your streak is crucial when tackling larger rooms packed with erratic adversaries. Practicing the cadence of lower-tempo tracks prepares you for higher-BPM chambers where enemy attack telegraphs overlap rapidly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the Heart of Artemisa protagonist deal bonus damage?
Bonus damage is achieved by executing attacks precisely on the beat of the soundtrack. Landing strikes during the "Perfect Beat" window grants significant damage multipliers, hit-stuns enemies, and charges the protagonist's special meter, whereas attacking off-beat penalizes damage output.

### What weapons can the Heart of Artemisa protagonist wield?
