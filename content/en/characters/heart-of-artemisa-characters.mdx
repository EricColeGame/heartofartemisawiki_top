## The Hero's Journey: Understanding Protagonist Mechanics and Rhythmic Movement

At the core of the game is your playable hero, an intrepid adventurer delving into the depths of a corrupted underground domain. Unlike conventional action roguelites where movement is continuous and arbitrary, your character's actions are bound to a strict metronome. Every tile navigated and every swing unleashed resonates with the active soundtrack.

When you attack directly on beat, your character deals substantial bonus damage, triggers critical hit multipliers, and reduces the recovery animation of weapons. Conversely, missing a beat can throw off your positioning, leaving your adventurer exposed to incoming projectiles or sweeping strikes.
