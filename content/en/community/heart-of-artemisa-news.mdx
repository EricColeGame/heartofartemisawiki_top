During the opening sequence, the boss executes telegraphed two- and three-hit strike combos. Keep your sound mix balanced so you can hear the percussive attack cues clearly.
* **Tactic:** Focus on parrying the second hit rather than dodging. A successful rhythm parry opens a vulnerability window for standard weapon combos.

* **Tactic:** Incorporate directional jump-dodges over low shockwaves, landing directly into down-strike punishers.

### Phase 3: Enrage and Desperation Rhythms
The final phase demands tight defensive execution. The arena narrows with rhythmic environmental hazards that activate on alternate measures.
* **Tactic:** Prioritize evasion until the boss concludes their extended flourish attack. Once the combo finishes, deploy your fully charged weapon skill to deplete the remaining health bar quickly.
