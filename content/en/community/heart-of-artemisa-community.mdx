## Rhythm Dungeoneering Tips: Mastering the Beat

Stepping into cursed catacombs where movement is bound to an audible pulse can feel disorienting at first. Drawing from collective community reports and veteran rhythm game tactics, these practical tips will help you preserve your health and maintain flawless combo strings.

### 1. Prioritize Audio Calibration Over Visuals
Visual cues like flashing borders or UI indicators are helpful, but human reaction time responds noticeably faster to auditory stimuli. If you notice your attacks consistently registering as "early" or "late," spend time calibrating your monitor's display latency and audio output device. Using wired headphones or low-latency speakers prevents Bluetooth lag from throwing off your strikes.

### 2. Learn Enemy Cadence Patterns
Every creature lurking in the catacombs operates on a set rhythmic loop. Ranged adversaries often fire projectiles every four beats, while aggressive swarming enemies hop forward on alternating beats. By tracking their musical intervals, you can dodge incoming threats without constantly looking at your character.

| Threat Category | Rhythmic Behavior | Counter-Strategy (Community Sourced) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crawling Vermin** | Advances one tile every quarter note | Hold ground and strike on the collision beat |
| **Skeleton Archers** | 3-beat draw animation, fires on beat 4 | Side-step on the third beat, close distance on beat 4 |
| **Armored Sentinels** | Shields raised on odd beats, open on even beats | Only commit to heavy attacks on even numbered downbeats |
| **Catacomb Boss (Phase 1)** | Sweeping cleaves spanning two consecutive bars | Dash away on the upbeat before the first cleave lands |
| **Catacomb Boss (Phase 2)** | Rapid off-beat shockwaves across room tiles | Stay centered and jump/roll precisely on syncopated off-beats |

### 3. Maintain Calm During Swarm Encounters
When multiple enemies crowd the screen, it is easy to panic and mash directional inputs. Button mashing breaks your combo chain, drastically lowers damage, and prevents recovery rolls. Treat combat encounters like a measured dance: focus on executing one on-beat action at a time. Retreating to narrow hallways limits enemy approach vectors and simplifies your timing windows.
