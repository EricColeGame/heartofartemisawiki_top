## Essential Survival Tips for High-BPM Encounters

- **Listen to Bass Cues over Visuals:** When visual clutter mounts during multi-mob fights, relying on your eyes alone can induce input latency. The underlying rhythm track's drum kicks and snare snaps align precisely with input windows.
- **Calibrate Your Audio Equipment:** Latency from Bluetooth headphones can ruin your timing against late-game heart of artemisa enemies. Opt for wired audio configurations whenever possible to keep input lag near zero.
- **Use Environmental Traps to Your Advantage:** Wall darts and floor spikes trigger on predictable musical bars. Lure heavily armored enemies into these traps to strip their health without expending your own weapon durability or stamina.
- **Prioritize Disruptors First:** Enemies that disrupt your movement—such as slowing webs or homing projectiles—should always be neutralized before engaging durable, slow-moving heavy units.
- **Don't Force Strikes on Off-Beats:** If an enemy telegraph overlaps with your attack window, prioritizing an evasive hop to an empty tile is always safer than trading blows at an off-beat damage penalty.
