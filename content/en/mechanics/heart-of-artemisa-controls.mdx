## Overview of Rhythm-Driven Mechanics

In *Heart of Artemisa*, your inputs do not merely trigger animations; they interact directly with an active metronome that governs the game world. Both player navigation and combat output are tethered to the underlying musical pulse. 

Striking an enemy or maneuvering on the half-beat or off-beat results in reduced effectiveness or missed opportunities. Conversely, maintaining a steady chain of on-beat commands multiplies your offensive output, extends weapon reach, and staggers aggressive enemies before they can close the distance.

```
Tempo Wave:  ♪  ---  [ Beat ]  ---  ♪  ---  [ Beat ]  ---  ♪
Input Zone:        [ Early ]   [ Perfect ]   [ Late ]
```

The game provides subtle visual and auditory anchors to help you internalize this tempo:
* **Audio Track Metronome:** The bassline and percussion accent the primary action beats.
* **Visual Pulsing:** Environmental torches, UI elements, and character indicators breathe in sync with incoming hit windows.
* **Haptic Rumble:** Supported gamepads deliver subtle vibrations just ahead of dominant beats.

## Troubleshooting Input Latency & Calibration Tips

Because *Heart of Artemisa* relies on tight millisecond-level precision, input lag is the single largest hurdle players face. Even a slight delay between pressing a button and the character reacting will cause repeated "Off-Beat" or "Miss" penalties.

Fine-tuning your **heart of artemisa controls** for zero input lag requires evaluating your entire hardware chain, from controller connection types to display refresh rates.

### Latency Reduction Checklist

Follow these optimization steps to ensure your hardware delivers pure, uninhibited rhythm responses:

| Component | Common Issue | Recommended Solution | Potential Latency Saved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Controller Link** | Bluetooth polling jitter | Switch to a direct USB-C or USB-A wired connection. | 8–20 ms |
| **Display Mode** | Post-processing delay | Enable "Game Mode" on TV/Monitor; turn off V-Sync in-game. | 15–40 ms |
| **Audio Output** | Wireless headphone latency | Use wired 3.5mm jacks or USB DACs instead of Bluetooth audio. | 50–150 ms |
| **Steam Input** | Layered configuration overhead | Set Steam Input to "Default Controller" without extra remapping layers. | 2–5 ms |
| **Game Calibration**| Mismatched AV sync | Run the built-in timing calibration tool to align visual hits to ear. | Varies by setup |

Taking five minutes before starting a run to run an audio calibration test guarantees your visual strikes align perfectly with the game's musical soundtrack.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I customize my Heart of Artemisa controls on PC?
Yes. PC players can fully rebind keyboard inputs directly through the in-game settings menu. For gamepads, players can modify button layouts either through native in-game settings or by leveraging the Steam Input configurator to remap triggers, face buttons, and stick sensitivity.

### Why do the Heart of Artemisa controls feel delayed on Bluetooth?
Standard Bluetooth connections introduce variable polling latency (often between 20 ms and 80 ms), which disrupts tight rhythm timing windows. Connecting your Xbox or DualSense controller via a direct USB cable eliminates wireless interference and stabilizes the input window for consistent "Perfect Beat" scores.

### Is it possible to play Heart of Artemisa effectively without a controller?
While Camacebra Games officially recommends a controller for the best gameplay feel, playing with a keyboard is completely viable. Using a mechanical keyboard with linear switches provides clean tactile feedback that helps keep time, especially during rapid dungeon clearing.

### What happens if I miss the beat while using combat controls?
Failing to time your inputs with the musical beat results in reduced strike damage, broken combo streaks, and slower character recovery. In extreme cases, swinging off-beat causes a brief stumble animation that leaves your character temporarily vulnerable to enemy counterattacks.
